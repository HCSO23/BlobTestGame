    How to play
    • Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or follow the mouse. Movement has momentum.
    • Eat every blob to stop the clock. Gold blobs are worth more. You get faster and the view widens as you grow.
    • Eat in quick succession to build a combo (up to ×2.6 Goo).
    • Buy upgrades on the move: 1–3 selects, ZXC buys.
    • Space dashes. A gold blob eaten mid-dash refunds the charge.
    • Checkpoints compare against your best run. The last 20 blobs are marked with arrows.
