    How to play

    • Your blob follows the mouse (or your finger).
    • Eat blobs for mass and Goo. Gold blobs are worth more.
    • Spend Goo on upgrades from the side panel (U on mobile).
    • Space dashes, E splits once you've bought them.
    • R restarts instantly, Esc returns to this menu.
    • Clear the map to stop the clock and earn Essence for permanent upgrades.
