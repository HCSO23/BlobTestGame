    How to play

    • Move with WASD / arrow keys, or follow the mouse (or your finger). You have momentum, so plan your turns.
    • Eat every blob to stop the clock. Gold blobs are worth more.
    • Eat quickly to build a combo: up to ×4 Goo. It drops if you go too long without eating.
    • Buy upgrades without stopping: 1–5 picks an ability, ZXC buys.
    • Space dashes. Dashes grab blobs from farther away, and eating a gold blob mid-dash refunds the charge.
    • E splits into pieces, Q regroups them early.
    • Your splits (every ⅙ of the map) are compared with your PB. R restarts, Esc opens this menu.
    • Clear the map to earn Essence for permanent upgrades.
